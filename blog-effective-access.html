Direct roles are not the whole access story

When two people with similar job titles can do different things in Dataverse, the answer often sits outside their direct role list.

Build the access chain

Start with the person's direct security roles, business unit, and team memberships. Team roles can add inherited privileges, while privilege depth affects where an operation is allowed. Looking at only one layer can produce a confident but incomplete answer.

Calculate effective access

Effective Access combines direct and inherited permissions. Filter the result to the table and operation under investigation, then trace the source. This is often faster than opening every role and team manually, but it should still be paired with the current business-unit and ownership context.

Compare a known-good peer

User comparison is useful when one person has the expected access and another does not. Compare their complete footprints rather than copying everything immediately. A difference may be intentional, and copying a highly privileged user's access can spread excessive permissions.

Check environment drift

Role names can match while privilege definitions differ. Use Role Diff to compare definitions between environments, and use team-role comparison when matching a team's assignments to production. Export the differences before approving any synchronization.

Good access-review evidence: direct roles, team memberships, effective privileges, business-unit context, comparison result, and the approved reason for any change.
